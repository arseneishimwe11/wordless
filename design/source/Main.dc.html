The pharmacist’s pen, made beautiful.

A medicine schedule anyone can follow without reading a word: the sun’s path for time, every pill drawn as it really looks, and a box for every dose.

Time is the color

Four inks, one for each moment the patient already knows. They darken as the day goes, so a black-and-white printer keeps the order — and every color always travels with its own symbol.
